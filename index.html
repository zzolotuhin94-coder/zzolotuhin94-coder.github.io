    The problem

    A resort village runs on dozens of small businesses — hotels, restaurants, ski lifts, taxis, event organisers — each with its own website, phone line and paper reservation book. Guests don't know what's open today, which lift is running or where there's a free table at 8 pm. Venues lose bookings to phone tag.

    The solution

    One platform with five parts that talk to each other in real time. A guest books a table → it appears on the restaurant’s floor plan → the host accepts → the guest gets a push notification. A guest orders a taxi → it lands in the dispatcher’s queue and on a driver’s phone → the guest watches the car arrive on a live map.

    Five parts, one system

    AlpenGo Guest — for visitors

    React Native · Expo · offline-first

    • Restaurants, hotels & apartments, events, news and a map with bus stops
    • Book a table, request a room, or order a taxi and follow it live on the map
    • Stripe payments, tips and ride ratings
    • Room service to your room, unlocked with a 6-digit stay code
    • “Fabi”, an AI concierge powered by Claude that knows the resort’s real partners
    • German, Dutch and English; email-code login and GDPR self-service

    AlpenGo Business — for venues

    React Native · TypeScript · Zustand

    • Restaurant, hotel and taxi modules on one unified dashboard
    • Interactive floor-plan editor, reservation timeline, waitlist
    • Hotel: rooms, pricing, iCal sync, housekeeping, room service
    • Digital guest registration with signature and tourist-tax count
    • Taxi: live dispatch, fleet, ride tiers, peak surcharges
    • AI-drafted review replies, Stripe subscriptions, five themes

    AlpenGo Driver — for taxi drivers

    React Native · Expo Location · Socket.io

    • Go online and share GPS every 20 seconds
    • Ride queue with accept / decline and equipment tags
    • One-tap status flow: arriving → on board → completed
    • Cash rides: mark payment as collected
    • Chat with the guest with DE / EN quick replies
    • History, stats and a licence & concession profile

    • Onboard businesses, switch modules per venue
    • Plan catalogue, per-venue fees, commission invoices
    • KPIs per destination, fleet overview across taxi companies
    • Promotions, broadcasts, bus stops and timetables

    Engineering highlights

    01

    Real-time from tap to table

    A booking travels guest app → API → PostgreSQL → Socket.io → partner app in well under a second. The host's floor plan lights up, the guest sees confirmed without refreshing.

    02

    A reservation state machine

    Six states — pending, confirmed, rejected, arrived, completed, cancelled — with explicit allowed transitions, time windows and capacity checks, so a table can't be double-booked for the same slot.

    03

    AI concierge that knows the valley

    Guests ask “where can we eat with kids tonight?” in their own language. The concierge answers from resort data through the Claude API, and conversations are stored and archived on the server.

    04

    Drag-and-drop floor plans

    Restaurants draw their own room: tables are dragged, resized and numbered on a canvas built with Reanimated and gesture handlers, then used for live occupancy during service.

    05

    One platform, three verticals

    Restaurants, hotels and taxi companies run on the same core. A business can own several venues, each with its own modules and subscription, all on one dashboard — and roles decide who configures and who only operates.

    06

    Dispatch in real time

    A taxi request reaches the dispatcher and every online driver at once. Drivers send GPS every 20 seconds and move the ride through a strict status flow, while the guest watches the car approach on a live map and chats with the driver.
